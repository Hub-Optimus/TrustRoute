Why it exists

The gap no one was solving

We tested two major OTAs end-to-end. Here's what we found — and why TrustRoute exists.

MakeMyTrip · Price
The same booking showed 3 different prices
₹2,010/night on the detail page. ₹9,000 at the review page. ₹4,551 at checkout. The math reconciles — but no user should have to figure that out.
"I would've abandoned at the review page — the price looked like it doubled." — Field test, Jun 2026
Booking.com · Ratings
4.1 "Disappointing" — but 5.0 on every subcategory
Staff 4.8, Facilities 5.0, Comfort 5.0, Value 5.0. The platform's own algorithm contradicted its own data. A user relying on the headline would skip a great property.
"If TrustRoute hadn't flagged this, I'd have never booked it." — Field test, Jun 2026
Booking.com · Amenities
No WiFi — never mentioned during checkout
The platform asked "Are you travelling for work?" and still didn't surface that the property has no internet. Buried three screens deep in the facilities section.
"I would've arrived at the hotel for a work trip with no WiFi and no warning." — Field test, Jun 2026
