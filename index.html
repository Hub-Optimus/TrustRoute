Why this exists

We tested two major OTAs.

These weren't edge cases. This was a single booking flow, start to finish. Every issue would have cost time, money, or both.

3 prices
Same room showed ₹2,010 on listing, ₹9,000 at review, ₹4,551 at checkout. The math reconciles — but no user should have to figure that out.
Booking.com Hidden ratings
