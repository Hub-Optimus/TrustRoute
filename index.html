Why TrustRoute

The gap no one was solving

OTAs handle the booking. Airlines handle the flight. But no one owns the space between your confirmation email and your actual trip. That's where things go wrong.

💸
Price changes mid-funnel
MakeMyTrip showed ₹2,010/night on the detail page. The checkout showed ₹4,551 total. We caught a 3-price funnel in one booking flow. You shouldn't have to notice this yourself.
⭐
Ratings that contradict themselves
Booking.com rated a hotel 4.1 "Disappointing" — but its subcategory scores were 5.0 across Comfort, Cleanliness, and Value. Their own algorithm contradicted itself.
📶
Critical info buried
A hotel asked "Are you travelling for work?" during checkout — and never surfaced that the property has no WiFi. TrustRoute found it in the facilities section. You would have arrived to no internet.
